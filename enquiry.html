Privacy Policy

Information we collect

OneSwitch SA may collect information submitted through website forms, including your name, email address, phone number, address details, suburb, city, service requirements and other relevant enquiry information.

Purpose of collection

We collect this information to respond to enquiries, understand customer needs, assess likely next steps, and direct follow-up in relation to fibre-related requests.

Consent

By submitting a form, you consent to OneSwitch SA contacting you regarding the request you have made. We will not use your information for unrelated marketing unless you separately opt in to additional contact.

Sharing

We do not sell personal information. Information may be shared only where necessary to support an enquiry, to communicate with a relevant provider or authorised source, or where required by law.

Retention and deletion

We do not specify a fixed retention period here. If you would like to request correction, deletion or further information about your personal data, please contact us by email and we will review the request in accordance with our internal process.
