About OneSwitch SA

Making fibre choice simpler, clearer and more accessible for South Africans.

Our story

OneSwitch SA was created to address a common problem: choosing the right fibre solution can be confusing, time-consuming and difficult to understand. Our goal is to simplify that journey by helping customers understand their options and make informed connectivity decisions.

Our mission

To simplify connectivity choices by guiding customers through relevant fibre enquiries and practical next steps.

Our vision

To become a trusted platform for helping South Africans explore suitable connectivity paths.

Our values

Trust, transparency, simplicity and customer-focused guidance.
