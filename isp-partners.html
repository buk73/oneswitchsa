Our Partnership Approach

OneSwitch SA (Pty) Ltd aims to make fibre connectivity choices simpler for South African homes and businesses. We welcome opportunities to engage with internet service providers and relevant connectivity partners.

Customer Enquiries

We help customers communicate their location, connectivity requirements and preferences so that relevant options can be explored.

Partner Collaboration

We seek to establish professional relationships with suitable providers to understand their service areas, packages, onboarding requirements and commercial terms.

Transparent Guidance

Our approach prioritises clear communication. Availability, pricing, installation and service conditions remain subject to the relevant provider.

Who We Want to Work With

We are interested in engaging with internet service providers, fibre network operators and other suitable connectivity-sector businesses.

Potential areas of collaboration may include customer referrals, lead handling, reseller arrangements and other mutually agreed commercial opportunities. Any arrangement would be subject to discussion, due diligence and a formal agreement where applicable.
