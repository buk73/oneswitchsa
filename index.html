Frequently Asked Questions

Is OneSwitch SA an internet provider?

No. OneSwitch SA is a fibre discovery, comparison and switching platform that helps customers identify suitable connectivity solutions from available providers.

How does OneSwitch SA work?

Customers provide their requirements and location. OneSwitch SA then helps them discover suitable connectivity options and understand the next steps.

Does it cost anything to request assistance?

Customers can submit a fibre recommendation request and receive guidance on available options.

Can businesses use OneSwitch SA?

Yes. OneSwitch SA supports both residential and business connectivity requirements.

Does OneSwitch SA guarantee fibre availability?

No. Fibre availability, pricing, installation, service speeds and other conditions are subject to the relevant fibre network and internet service provider.
