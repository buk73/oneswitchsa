Frequently Asked Questions

Is OneSwitch SA an internet service provider?

No. OneSwitch SA is an independent fibre discovery, comparison and switching platform that helps customers identify suitable connectivity solutions from available providers.

How does OneSwitch SA work?

Customers provide their requirements and location. OneSwitch SA then helps them identify suitable connectivity options and understand the next steps.

Is there a cost to request assistance?

Customers may submit a fibre recommendation request and receive guidance regarding available options.

Can businesses use OneSwitch SA?

Yes. OneSwitch SA supports both residential and business connectivity requirements.

Does OneSwitch SA guarantee fibre availability?

No. Fibre availability, pricing, installation, service speeds and other conditions are determined by the relevant fibre network and internet service provider.
