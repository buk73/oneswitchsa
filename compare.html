How OneSwitch SA works

A straightforward three-step process for identifying a suitable fibre path.

1. Share your requirements

Provide your location, preferred connection type, speed expectations and budget.

2. Review relevant options

OneSwitch SA helps identify likely avenues for follow-up based on your information.

3. Confirm through the provider

Availability, installation and package details must be confirmed with the relevant provider or authorised source.
