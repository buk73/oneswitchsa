About OneSwitch SA

Making fibre choice simpler, clearer and more accessible for South Africans.

Our Story

OneSwitch SA was created to solve a common problem: choosing the right fibre solution can be confusing, time-consuming and difficult to understand.

Our goal is to simplify that journey by helping customers understand their options and make informed connectivity decisions.

Our Mission

To simplify connectivity choices by connecting customers with suitable fibre solutions through a trusted and customer-focused platform.

Our Vision

To become South Africa's trusted platform for discovering, comparing and switching connectivity solutions.

Our Values

Trust, transparency, innovation and putting customers first guide everything we build.

Why OneSwitch SA Exists

Simplifying Complexity

Technology should make life easier, not create more confusion.

Customer Empowerment

Customers deserve clear information before making connectivity decisions.

Future Ready

We are building a platform designed to grow with the changing connectivity landscape.

The Future of OneSwitch SA

Our long-term vision includes smarter technology, improved customer experiences and tools that make finding connectivity solutions faster and easier.
